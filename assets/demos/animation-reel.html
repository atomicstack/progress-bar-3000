progress-bar-3000 / eight animations

colour, in motion.

real cli recordings, rendered with --style granular --color-mode truecolor. each lossless rgb animation loops for ten seconds at approximately 15 frames per second. select a preview to open its full-size animated png.

the six ambient effects hold at 68% for four seconds, then fill to completion. the two event effects use discrete milestone and phase updates so their triggers are easy to see.

01 / aurora

soft colour bands drift through the fill. compare the movement while progress holds steady.

./progress-bar-3000 --socket-path /tmp/pb3-demo.sock --color-mode truecolor --fps 30 --width 54 --format '%p %{percent}' --style granular --gradient-start '#ff70d2' --gradient-end '#00d8ff' --tint-animation aurora

commands show the recording flags with a reusable example socket path. the recorder sends @set-phases fetch,build,test,package before other events, sets the total to 100, then sends the real progress or phase events shown above. use a separate socket path for each simultaneous renderer.

re-record all eight from the repository root with python scripts/record-demos.py --only animation-reel after building the cli and installing pillow and pyte. this gallery works directly from disk.
